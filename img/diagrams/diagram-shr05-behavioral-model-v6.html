Motivators
  • Sharing content that reflects identity or taste
  • Sparking conversation with friends or groups
  • Saving or bookmarking for later reference
Inhibitors
  • Fear of exposing pseudonymous identity
  • Uncertainty about what info travels with the share
  • Raw URLs look untrustworthy to recipients
User perception
  • Reddit URLs perceived as unfamiliar or sketchy
  • No context about what to expect after tapping
  • Screenshots more trusted than links
