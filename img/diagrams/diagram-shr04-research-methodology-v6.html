Senders’ perceptions of sharing
“They’re not on Reddit”
Many users reported that friends weren’t on Reddit, and didn’t believe they’d visit to see content.
“They won’t get it”
Users didn’t believe sharing flows gave enough context for non-Redditors to appreciate the content.
“A screenshot will work”
Users believed taking a screenshot captured context most quickly for sharing.
Recipients’ perceptions of receiving
“I’m not a Redditor”
Non-Redditors who received shared content didn’t care to engage with the platform.
“I won’t click this”
Users who received Reddit links were unsure what they were looking at and felt distrustful.
“Where am I?”
Users unfamiliar with Reddit who received shared content felt overwhelmed landing on the platform.
